The Team

Operators first.

The pattern recognition that drives every Silver Field decision comes from a decade and a half of doing the work — buying, integrating, scaling, and exiting founder-led businesses across the lower middle market.

SH
Founder & Managing Partner

Shamus Hines

Shamus founded Silver Field Capital to do at the firm level what he spent more than a decade doing at the company level: identify undervalued, founder-led businesses in overlooked markets, and build them into platforms that endure.

He began his career in institutional investment management, where he developed the analytical foundation that still drives how Silver Field evaluates businesses. As a CFA charterholder working across research and advisory roles at UBS and Morningstar, he studied hundreds of companies across the middle market — building pattern recognition for what durable businesses actually look like underneath the surface: recurring revenue with real retention, products embedded in workflows customers can't easily replace, and founder-owners who built something worth preserving.

He put that pattern recognition to work as the architect of Upshop's buy-and-build strategy. Over more than a decade, Shamus identified, acquired, and integrated six founder-led vertical SaaS businesses serving grocery and convenience retailers — navigating seller psychology, deal structure, cultural integration, product migration, and team building at every step. He led the platform's transition from on-premise software to SaaS, built a professional executive team from scratch, and scaled the business to approximately $60 million in ARR before recapitalizing with institutional investors. He continues to serve on the Upshop board.

Beyond Upshop, Shamus has served in board and advisory capacities across the lower middle market, compounding a perspective on what separates businesses that scale from businesses that stall — and what it actually takes to close that gap.

He is based in East Grand Rapids, Michigan, with his wife Meg and their family. He coaches youth baseball, is active in his faith community, and believes West Michigan is one of the most underestimated pools of quietly great companies in the country.

JR
Investment Associate

John Rietema

John leads Silver Field's sourcing efforts and is a core member of the investment team. He is the first voice many founders, brokers, and intermediaries hear from us — and the throughline from initial conversation through diligence and close.

A Grand Rapids native, John brings a finance background built across three distinct corners of the capital markets. At Cinnaire, he worked inside one of the Midwest's leading community development financial institutions, where disciplined underwriting and patient capital intersect with real economic outcomes — a foundation that maps directly onto how Silver Field thinks about long-horizon investing. At Oxford Companies, he gained operational and financial experience inside an asset-intensive, multi-property business — the kind of environment where attention to detail compounds and where cash flow discipline is the difference between good years and great ones. Earlier in his career, he held finance roles at Liberty University.

At Silver Field, John pairs that finance discipline with the breadth and pace required to run a serious deal pipeline. He owns the firm's sourcing motion end to end — broker and intermediary relationships, proprietary outreach, qualification, and the structured handoff into diligence — and partners with Shamus across the investment process from first conversation to closing.

John is a graduate of Grand Valley State University. He lives in West Michigan and brings a deep network across the Grand Rapids business and capital community to every Silver Field conversation.
