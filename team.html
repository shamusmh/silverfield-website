Founder & Managing Partner

Shamus Hines

Shamus founded Silver Field to do at the firm level what he spent fifteen years doing at the company level: acquire undervalued, founder-led businesses in overlooked markets and build them into platforms that endure.

His operating career began in institutional investment management — first at UBS, then at Morningstar — where he spent nearly a decade in analytical and strategic growth roles supporting the wealth management industry. He left to pursue an MBA at the University of Chicago Booth School of Business, graduating with honors, with a clear intention: to own and operate businesses.

What followed was more than a decade of hands-on platform building — acquiring a founder-led lower middle market business and executing a buy-and-build strategy across multiple acquisitions, a major transformative merger, and two institutional recapitalizations with growth equity partners. Shamus has led or co-led the acquisition, integration, and scaling of multiple companies, grown a platform from a small software business into a category leader recognized globally, and developed a repeatable playbook for what it actually takes to compound enterprise value in founder-led businesses.

He continues to serve on the board of the platform he built and brings that operating experience — not just pattern recognition, but the scar tissue — to every Silver Field investment.

Shamus is a graduate of the University of Michigan and holds an MBA with honors from the University of Chicago Booth School of Business. He is based in East Grand Rapids, Michigan, with his wife Meg and their family. He coaches youth baseball, is active in his faith community, and believes West Michigan is one of the most underestimated pools of quietly great companies in the country.
