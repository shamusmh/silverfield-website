The pattern recognition that drives every Silver Field decision comes from a decade and a half of doing the work — buying, integrating, scaling, and exiting founder-led businesses across the lower middle market.

Founder & Managing Partner

Shamus Hines

Shamus founded Silver Field to do at the firm level what he spent more than a decade doing at the company level: identify undervalued, founder-led businesses in overlooked markets, and build them into platforms that endure.

Shamus has served in operating, board, and advisory capacities across the middle market, compounding a perspective on what separates businesses that scale from businesses that stall — and what it actually takes to close that gap.

He is based in East Grand Rapids, Michigan, with his wife Meg and their family. He coaches youth baseball, is active in his faith community, and believes West Michigan is one of the most underestimated pools of quietly great companies in the country.
