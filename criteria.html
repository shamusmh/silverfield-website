What We Look For

Durable businesses.
Built to compound.

We're disciplined about the businesses we partner with. The pattern that works for us is straightforward — and recognizing it early lets us move quickly when we see it.

Core Criteria

What a fit looks like.

Five attributes describe nearly every business we've owned, scaled, and exited. They're not all equally weighted — but the strongest opportunities have most of them, and the truly exceptional have all five.

Recurring Revenue

Contracted, subscription, or otherwise predictable revenue that customers renew because they have to — not because we ask them to. We strongly favor businesses where revenue compounds without restarting the sales cycle every year.

Subscription, contracted, or embedded service revenue

Consistent Profitability

Double-digit EBITDA margins, sustained across cycles. We're not looking for businesses we have to fix — we're looking for businesses that already work, with room to compound when the right operating discipline is applied on top.

Double-digit EBITDA margins, year over year

Defensible Moat

Workflow embedded software, regulatory positioning, network effects, switching costs, deep customer integration, proprietary data, or a brand customers actively prefer. Something real that keeps competitors from showing up tomorrow.

Switching costs, embedment, or brand

High Client Retention

Logo retention well above category benchmarks, low involuntary churn, and net revenue retention above 100% where the model supports it. Retention is the single best leading indicator of compounding enterprise value.

Logo retention 90%+; ideally NRR > 100%

Industry Focus

Where we go deep.

Two adjacent categories where our operating playbook compounds most directly — and where we've already done the work.

Vertical Software

Mission-critical software embedded in the workflow of a specific industry — where switching is hard, retention is high, and the path to multi-product expansion is real. Our roots are here.

  • Workflow software with multi-year contracts
  • Modernization opportunity (on-prem → SaaS)
  • Fragmented competitive landscape inviting roll-up

Business Services

Service businesses with recurring or contracted revenue, professional management, and the kind of operating leverage that comes from process discipline, technology adoption, and disciplined M&A.

  • Contracted or repeat-customer revenue
  • Tech-enabled or productized services
  • Roll-up potential within a region or vertical
Situations

Founder situations we serve well.

Not every founder is selling for the same reason — and the right structure depends on the right reason. Here are the situations where we're at our most useful.

Succession & Stewardship

You've built something worth preserving and you want a partner who'll treat it that way. We can buy 100%, install a CEO, and protect the legacy you've built — without forcing a fund-clock exit five years from now.

Partial Liquidity, Continued Upside

You want to take chips off the table now but stay involved for the next chapter. We'll structure a deal that gives you meaningful cash today and meaningful equity in a bigger outcome down the road.

Growth Capital with an Operator

The business is working — you need capital, M&A capacity, and an operating partner who's done it before to take the next step. Deal-by-deal capital and an embedded operator are the package.

Platform for a Roll-Up

You're sitting in a fragmented market and you can see the consolidation opportunity — but executing it is a full-time job that's bigger than the one you signed up for. We'll fund and execute it alongside you.

Where We Don't Fit

Being honest about what we're not for.

We're not a fit for early-stage venture, turnaround situations requiring rescue capital, or businesses where the value lives entirely in a single founder's relationships. We look for businesses with their own legs — where our operating playbook and patient capital can make a working business into a category leader.
