Option A — IBM Plex Serif + Plex Sans

Engineered, institutional

IBM Plex was designed for IBM as a corporate typeface — it reads as technical, precise, and established. Not a font you'll see on AI-generated marketing sites. The serif headings feel like a research house; the sans body feels like documentation.

Option B — Spectral + Archivo

Bookish serif, industrial sans

Spectral is a screen-first serif commissioned by Google / Production Type — warm and readable, with none of the high-contrast "fashion serif" look of Fraunces. Archivo is a grotesque sans with an industrial, almost newsprint character. Together they feel editorial and grounded.
